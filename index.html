A practical route to selection

Most bank recruitment tests move from speed-heavy prelims to broader mains and then, for officer posts, an interview or language/proficiency stage. Always match the latest notice for your exact post.

12-week build

1
Weeks 1–3 · Accuracy

Learn arithmetic, core grammar and basic puzzle types. Solve untimed, write the reason behind every error.

2
Weeks 4–7 · Speed

Add approximation, calculation drills, timed sets and daily reading. Start banking terms and current-affairs notes.

3
Weeks 8–10 · Sectionals

Alternate sectional tests with repair sessions. Measure attempts, accuracy and time lost—not only score.

4
Weeks 11–12 · Full mocks

Rehearse the exact order and time limits in the current notification. Keep the day before a mock for revision, not new topics.

Banking & general awareness

A compact foundation in institutions, monetary policy, payments, accounts and common financial terms. Pair it with recent monthly current affairs for the actual exam window.

Core banking concepts

Repo rate
Rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to banks against eligible collateral. A higher rate generally makes borrowing costlier.
SDF
Standing Deposit Facility: RBI absorbs liquidity from banks without providing collateral.
CRR
Cash Reserve Ratio: share of a bank’s net demand and time liabilities kept as cash with RBI.
SLR
Statutory Liquidity Ratio: share maintained in liquid assets such as cash, gold and approved securities.
NPA
A loan asset where interest or principal remains overdue beyond the regulatory threshold; for standard term loans, the commonly tested threshold is more than 90 days.
Priority sector
Directed lending categories such as agriculture, MSMEs, education, housing and weaker sections, under RBI norms.

Payments & inclusion

UPI
Instant account-to-account payments using a virtual payment address, mobile number or QR, operated by NPCI.
IMPS
Immediate interbank electronic transfer available round the clock.
NEFT
Electronic funds transfer settled in half-hourly batches and available 24×7.
RTGS
Real-time, transaction-by-transaction gross settlement, mainly for high-value transfers.
AEPS
Aadhaar Enabled Payment System supports basic banking through Aadhaar authentication and business correspondents.
DICGC
Deposit insurance corporation covering eligible deposits in insured banks, subject to the prevailing statutory limit and conditions.

Institutions

RBI
India’s central bank: monetary policy, currency issuance, reserves, payment systems and bank regulation.
NABARD
Apex development financial institution focused on agriculture and rural development.
SIDBI
Principal financial institution for promotion, financing and development of the MSME sector.
SEBI
Regulates India’s securities market and protects investor interests.
IRDAI
Regulates and develops the insurance sector in India.
PFRDA
Regulates and develops the pension sector, including the National Pension System.

Accounts, credit & risk

CASA
Current Account Savings Account deposits; usually a lower-cost funding source for banks than term deposits.
MCLR
Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate, an internal benchmark for certain floating-rate loans.
Credit score
A summary indicator of credit behaviour; lenders also use income, obligations and their own underwriting rules.
Basel norms
Global standards covering bank capital, risk and liquidity. Capital buffers absorb unexpected losses.
KYC
Customer-identification and due-diligence process used to reduce fraud, money laundering and misuse.
Phishing
Fraudulent attempt to obtain credentials through deceptive messages or sites. Banks do not need your PIN or OTP to receive money.

Exam-room strategy

The most useful plan is one you have already rehearsed in full mocks.

Use a two-pass method

First solve direct questions. Mark lengthy but solvable items for a second pass. Do not let one puzzle consume a section.

Protect accuracy

If two options remain and you cannot prove one, move on. Negative marking changes the value of a doubtful attempt.

Read the stem twice

Words such as “not”, “except”, “least” and “incorrect” reverse the task. Circle them mentally before calculating.

Analyze every mock

Classify errors as concept, calculation, reading, selection or time. The repair depends on the category.

Build current affairs by theme

Group facts under banking, economy, national, international, appointments, awards and reports. Revise through questions.

For descriptive papers

Use a clear opening, two or three developed points, and a balanced conclusion. Practice typing within a timer.
